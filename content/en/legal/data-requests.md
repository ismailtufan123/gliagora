## About this page

This page explains how to use your rights over the personal data that Gliagora | Neuroscience holds about you. The data controller is Nuray Sancar, who runs Gliagora. The [privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy/) explains what data we process, why and for how long. In Turkish, see the [KVKK information notice ({tr: aydınlatma metni})](/tr/legal/kvkk-aydinlatma-metni/).

## Your rights in short

You can ask us whether we hold data about you and ask for a copy of it. You can ask us to correct it or delete it. If you are in the European Union, you can also ask us to limit how we use it. Where we rely on our legitimate interest, you can object (see "[Your right to object](/en/legal/privacy/#your-right-to-object)"). You can withdraw your consent at any time. The full lists of your rights under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) are under "[Your rights](/en/legal/privacy/#your-rights)" in the privacy policy. ## Do it yourself, at once

- **Unsubscribe from Glia Notes:** use the link in the welcome email or any issue (it opens a page with one button), or your mail program's unsubscribe button. It takes effect at once, and your address is deleted from the newsletter list then. Members can also use the newsletter switch in their account settings: it stops Glia Notes at once, and the address is deleted from the newsletter list within minutes (usually at once).
- **Download your data (members):** "Download my data" in your account settings gives you a JSON file, a common file format.
- **Delete your account (members):** use "Delete my account" in your account settings. When you confirm, we delete your account, saved items and settings at once, and end the Glia Notes subscription of its address (also one made on the newsletter page). The consent records of that subscription lose the newsletter software's number and code for it and are kept for their 3 years (see "Data we cannot find"). If your account has an email address, we then send you one final email to confirm the deletion. We record the deletion as a request (see "Records of your request").

## Delete your data with the form

You do not need an account to have your data deleted. Members can use the form too.

1. In the [form on this page](#request-form), choose "Delete my data" and enter your email address.
2. We send a link to that address. It works for 24 hours and only once.
3. When you open it and confirm, we delete at once:
   - each suggestion you sent with that address, with its link, note and address;
   - a Gliagora account with that address, if there is one, with its saved items and settings;
   - the newsletter subscription of that address, if there is one;
   - your address on the problem reports and corrections you sent with it.

   We also remove the newsletter software's number and code from the consent records of that subscription; the records themselves stay for their 3 years (see "Data we cannot find"). Then we send you one email that says what we deleted.

Requests about your personal rights that you sent through "Report a problem" keep your address: we handle them under Turkish Law No. 5651 and keep them for 2 years after they are closed, to be able to show how we handled them. Whether you use this form or delete your account, some records stay for the periods set in the privacy policy: the records of your requests, our email correspondence with you and our logs. Deleted data, including a deleted account, disappears from our backups within 6 months. If we ever restore a backup, we delete it again.

## Other requests

For anything else, such as a correction, an objection or a copy of your data if you are not a member, use the [form on this page](#request-form) or write to [privacy@gliagora.com](mailto:privacy@gliagora.com). In the form, enter the email address you use with Gliagora, choose what you are asking for and describe it under "Details". Your name is optional: give it if it helps us find your data. If you write by email, tell us your name, the email address you use with Gliagora and what you are asking for, and write from that address. You can also email us a request signed with a secure electronic or mobile signature. You can write in English or Turkish. If you use the form, we email you at once. This email is our acknowledgement, and it asks you to confirm the request with a link that works for 24 hours. We act only on confirmed requests. Once you confirm a request, we answer it free of charge within 30 days of receiving it under KVKK (Art. 13), or within one month under the GDPR (Art. 12). [TO DECIDE: B10 – answer period] If we refuse a request, we explain why.

To have content about you corrected or removed from a listing, use the [notice and takedown page](/en/legal/takedown/).

## How we check that a request is yours

Before we act, we check that the request comes from you, so that no one else can see or delete your data. Usually the link we send to your email address is enough: only the person who reads that mailbox can confirm the request. If it is not, we may ask you for more information, but only what we need for this check. ## Data we cannot find

- **Consent records** name a subscription only by the newsletter software's number and random code for it, never by your email address. While you are subscribed, we find them through your address in the newsletter software. If you are a member, your account names the subscription it holds, and "Download my data" includes that subscription's consent records: the one you switched on in your account, or the one your account's address has through the newsletter page, which your account takes over within minutes after you create the account or add that address. Records of a subscription that has ended (you unsubscribed, or switched it off) no longer lead to your address in our live systems, unless you are a member: your account keeps when you last changed Glia Notes, which is also the time of a record. After a deletion, the records of the subscription it ended lose the number and the code; the record of your request, kept for 2 years with your address (see "Records of your request"), shows when that happened. - **Visitor statistics** are counted without cookies. We cannot tell which records, if any, come from your visits. [TO DECIDE: B14 – fields of a raw Umami event, and whether they are personal data]

If you can give us details that would let us find such a record, we will check.

## Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain:

- **in Türkiye,** to the [Personal Data Protection Board](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr) ({tr: Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu}), after you have sent your request to us (KVKK Art. 14). If we reject it, if you find our answer insufficient, or if we do not answer in time, you can complain within 30 days of learning our answer, and in any case within 60 days of the date of your request. [TO DECIDE: B10 – KVKK complaint periods]
- **in the European Union,** to the data protection authority of the country where you live, where you work or where you think the problem happened (GDPR Art. 77).

## Records of your request

We keep a record of each request: its type, your email address, your name if you give it, the details you give us, the date we received it, the date our answer is due, the date we closed it, its status and our email correspondence with you. For a deletion done through the form, we keep neither your name nor the details, but we keep how many records were deleted. We keep the record for 2 years after the request is closed, in our database and our mailbox, to be able to show how we handled it.

If you send the form but do not confirm the request, we keep only its type, the date and a scrambled code made from your email address (a hash), for 60 days, to be able to show that a request arrived. We do not keep the address itself. What you wrote in the form is kept only with the link, and is deleted with it after 24 hours.
