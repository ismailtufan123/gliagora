## About this page

Gliagora | Neuroscience collects positions in neuroscience, and will add events, funding, papers, news and resources step by step. This page explains where our items come from, how we check and write them, what the site does by itself, and how to get something corrected. It is the longer version of "How curation works" on the [About page](/en/about/).

## What an item is

Every item on Gliagora has three parts:

- **the key facts**, such as the employer, the place, the type of position and the deadline, each checked on the original page;
- **a short summary in our own words**;
- **a link to the original page**, where you apply, register or read on.

We do not copy descriptions, abstracts, images or logos. We do not take applications, charge fees or act between you and an employer. The [terms of use](/en/legal/terms/) say more about this.

## Where items come from

| Source | What we use it for | How we use it |
|---|---|---|
| Employers' own pages: universities, research institutes and companies | Positions | Every listing rests on the employer's own page. Our editor reads that page and links to it. |
| Job boards, the boards of scientific societies, mailing lists and social media | Finding positions | Only to learn that a position exists. We then look for it on the employer's own page and link there. We publish nothing from a board's own listing, and we never copy a board in bulk. |
| Your suggestions ([send us one](/en/suggest/)) | Positions | Our editor checks each suggestion on the original page. A suggestion is never published automatically. |
| The Research Organization Registry (ROR) | The names, places and identifiers of institutions | Taken from ROR's public data, which is free to reuse (CC0). |
| OpenStreetMap | The map, and the time zone in which a deadline ends | See "Data and credits" on the [About page](/en/about/). |

We list only positions that employers advertise themselves. We do not list advertisements placed by recruitment agencies.

## How we check

Before an item is published, our editor checks that:

- the original page can be reached, and it is the employer's own;
- the position is still open, and its deadline is the one on the original page;
- the item has at least one subfield and a type of position;
- the summary is our own, with no copied sentences;
- no personal contact details are copied: you contact the employer through the original page;
- there are no logos or images.

We publish a deadline or a condition only after we have seen it on the original page. Each item shows the date we last verified it. A position leaves the lists by itself when its deadline has passed. The deadline day ends in the time zone of the institution, unless the posting names another.

If our item and the original page differ, the original page is correct. Please tell us, so that we can correct the item.

Items sometimes name people, for example the head of a lab, as the original page does. The [privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy/#people-named-in-listings-and-sources) explains how we handle those names.

## What the site does by itself

A person reads, checks, writes and approves every item. Today the site does only these things by itself:

- it takes a position out of the lists when its deadline has passed;
- it brings in the public list of research institutions from ROR;
- it sends the emails that answer a form or a request.

Our rule for sources: a program may read a source by itself only if the source is an official feed or interface that its owner offers for that purpose, and its terms of use and its `robots.txt` file allow it. A person reads everything else. Job boards are never read by a program or copied in bulk, whatever their terms say. Today no program reads any source of positions.

## AI tools

Today no AI tool chooses, writes or checks the items we publish. [TO DECIDE: B17 – AI drafting stays switched off until the lawyer review has answered it]

If we switch AI drafting on, it will work like this:

- an AI service (the Claude API, by Anthropic) may prepare a first draft of a summary, pick out facts such as the deadline, and suggest tags;
- before any text is sent, email addresses, phone numbers and personal names are replaced with placeholders. For papers, the author lists and contact details are removed;
- our editor checks every fact against the original page, edits the text and approves it. Nothing is published without that approval, and our editor stays responsible for what is published;
- from that day on, this page will say that AI drafting is in use.

## Corrections

If you find a mistake, tell us. Every item has a "Report a problem" link, and the [notice and takedown page](/en/legal/takedown/) lists every way to reach us and how quickly we act.

- We correct a fact as soon as we have checked it on the original page. The item's "Last verified" date then shows that day.
- We take a listing offline at once when the position is withdrawn.
- We keep a record of changes and of requests, as the notice and takedown page describes.

## Employers

Employers cannot edit items themselves. Nobody needs our permission to be listed, and nobody pays for it. If you write for an employer:

- **To change a fact,** update your own page first, then tell us. We change the item once we have seen the change there.
- **To withdraw a position,** tell us, and we take the listing offline at once.
- **To suggest a position,** [send it to us](/en/suggest/).
- **If you do not want a position listed,** ask us to remove the listing and tell us why.

The [notice and takedown page](/en/legal/takedown/#employers-updates-and-removals) has the details.

## Independence, payment and sponsorship

- No one pays to appear on Gliagora, and no one can pay for a better place in a list. Lists are ordered by deadline or by date, and search results by how well they match your words.
- Gliagora takes no money from the employers, funders, publishers or organisers it lists. At present, it carries no advertising or sponsorship.
- If we ever add sponsorship, we will label sponsored content clearly. It will not change which items we list or how we describe them, and we will update this page and the terms of use first.
