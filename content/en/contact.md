## What Gliagora is

Gliagora | Neuroscience (gliagora.com) is a hand-picked neuroscience hub, published in English first. We are building one place for neuroscience positions, events, funding, papers, news and resources. We open sections step by step. The menu lists every section, and a section that is not open yet says so on its page. Glia Notes is the monthly Gliagora newsletter.

For each position, we publish a listing with the key facts, a short summary in our own words and a link to the employer's page. On this page, a **position** is a job, a **listing** is our page about it and the **employer's page** is the original advertisement on the employer's website.

We charge no fees, and no one can pay to have a listing shown or promoted. At present, we carry no advertising or sponsorship.

## Not an employment agency

We publish information about open positions. We are not an employment agency. We do not act for employers or applicants, and we do not act as an intermediary in job placement. In Türkiye, anyone who acts as an intermediary in job placement, including online, needs permission from the Turkish Employment Agency (İŞKUR) under Law No. 4904. - We list only positions that employers, such as universities, institutes or companies, advertise on their own websites. We do not list positions advertised by recruitment agencies.
- We do not collect CVs or applications, we do not match people to positions and we do not charge anyone a fee.
- Employers cannot add listings to Gliagora directly. Anyone can [suggest a listing](/en/suggest/), and our editor decides whether to publish it.
- You apply on the employer's page. Always check the details there: if our listing and the employer's page differ, the employer's page is correct.

## Who is responsible for the content

Gliagora has one editor, the person who runs it. Our editor writes our summaries, checks every listing against the employer's page and approves it before it goes live. Nothing is published without that check, including listings that visitors suggest.

Under Türkiye's internet law (Law No. 5651), our editor is the "content provider" ({tr: içerik sağlayıcı}): the person responsible for what we publish. [TO DECIDE: B1 – content provider role] Our editor is also the data controller named in the [privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy/).

- At present, we do not publish anything written by visitors or members.
- We link to employers' pages and other sources. Those pages belong to their owners. A link does not mean we endorse the employer or the page.

For now, this page gives email addresses only. Before Gliagora earns any income, we will add to this page the full name (or company name) and the postal address of whoever runs it. [TO DECIDE: B1 – email addresses only at launch; details and address needed before any income]

## How to ask for a correction or removal

Tell us if something we published is wrong or out of date, or if it affects your personal rights (for example, your privacy or your reputation). You can:

- use the **Report a problem** link on the listing or page;
- fill in the form on our notice and takedown page, our page for correction and removal requests;
- email [hello@gliagora.com](mailto:hello@gliagora.com).

The **Report a problem** link and the notice and takedown form open step by step, as we build Gliagora. Until they are open, email [hello@gliagora.com](mailto:hello@gliagora.com).

Include the link to the page, what is wrong and what you would like us to do. Employers can ask us to update or remove their own listings in the same way. If you use the form on the notice and takedown page, you get an automatic email as soon as we receive your request.

If content affects your personal rights, you can ask us to remove it (Law No. 5651, Article 9). You can also apply directly to a criminal judgeship of peace ({tr: sulh ceza hâkimliği}) to have the content removed or access to it blocked. [TO DECIDE: B2 – direct application to a court]

We confirm within 24 hours that we have received a request about your personal rights. We usually decide within 72 hours. [TO DECIDE: B2 – legal deadline for a reply or a decision] If we remove a listing or page, it goes offline at once. If the problem can be fixed, we may correct it instead.

## How to make a request about your personal data

You can ask to see your personal data, get a copy of it, correct it or delete it, or object to how we use it. Requests are free. We answer within 30 days. If the EU GDPR applies to you, we answer within one month, even when that is shorter than 30 days. [TO DECIDE: B10 – answer period]

- **If you have a Gliagora account:** download your data or delete your account at any time in your account settings.
- **If you receive Glia Notes:** unsubscribe with one click from any Glia Notes email.
- **For any other request, or if you have no account:** use the data-request page or email [privacy@gliagora.com](mailto:privacy@gliagora.com).

Accounts and the data-request page open step by step, as we build Gliagora. Until the data-request page is open, email [privacy@gliagora.com](mailto:privacy@gliagora.com).
