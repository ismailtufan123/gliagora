## About this page

This page explains how to ask Gliagora | Neuroscience to correct or remove something we have published, for example a wrong fact or content that harms your personal rights, such as your privacy or your reputation.

Our editor is responsible for every item, also for those that an AI assistant drafted and checked against our written rules in a publishing round our editor started. Under Turkish Law No. 5651, our editor is the content provider ({tr: içerik sağlayıcı}), responsible for what we publish. [TO DECIDE: B1 – content provider role] In Turkish, the main points are on the [{tr: İletişim ve yasal bilgiler}](/tr/iletisim/) page.

## How to reach us

- **"Report a problem":** every item has this link. It opens a short form with the item already filled in. Use it for a wrong fact, a broken link or a closed position. The form asks what is wrong, for the details and for your email address. The email address is optional, except for a request about your personal rights, which needs it so that we can reply.
- **Without an item:** [report it to us](/en/report/), also for corrections, removals and requests about your personal rights. Through the report form, if you give your email address, you get an automatic email as soon as we receive your request.
- **Email:** [hello@gliagora.com](mailto:hello@gliagora.com), in English or Turkish.

For a request about your personal rights, [report it to us](/en/report/) with your email address, so that we can reply.

To see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, such as your newsletter address, use the [data-request page](/en/legal/data-requests/) or write to [privacy@gliagora.com](mailto:privacy@gliagora.com).

## What to include

Please give us:

- the address of the page on Gliagora;
- what is wrong, or which of your rights is affected, and why;
- what you ask for: a correction or a removal;
- an email address where we can reach you.

If you write for an employer or another person, say so. Please do not send sensitive data, such as information about your health.

## How quickly we act

- **Personal rights:** we confirm within 24 hours that we have received your request. We usually decide within 72 hours. [TO DECIDE: B2 – legal deadline for a reply or a decision]
- **Wrong facts:** we correct a fact as soon as we have checked it on the original posting.

Once we decide to remove an item, it goes offline at once. If a correction is enough, we correct the item instead. If your request is also about your personal data under KVKK or the GDPR, the answer periods in the [privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy/) apply as well.

## Your personal rights

If something on Gliagora violates your personal rights, you can ask us to remove it (Law No. 5651, Article 9). You can also apply directly to a criminal judgeship of peace ({tr: sulh ceza hâkimliği}) to have it removed or access to it blocked. [TO DECIDE: B2 – direct application to a court]

If you are named in an item, for example as the head of a lab, you can ask us to correct or delete your name. You can also object to this use of your name (see "[Your right to object](/en/legal/privacy/#your-right-to-object)" in the privacy policy). ## Employers: updates and removals

Employers cannot edit listings on Gliagora themselves. Ask us instead:

- **Update:** we change a fact, such as a deadline, only after we have seen it on your own page. Please update that page first.
- **Withdrawn position:** tell us, and we take the listing offline at once.
- **Removal for another reason:** if you do not want a position listed on Gliagora, ask us to remove the listing and tell us why.

## What happens to a removed listing

- It no longer appears in lists or search results on Gliagora.
- Its address shows a short page saying that the listing has been removed. Search engines are told that the page is gone, but may show it for a short time.
- We keep the full record for 12 months, then only its web address and ID, so that the address keeps showing that the listing was removed.

## If you disagree

If we refuse your request, or grant only part of it, reply to our answer and tell us why. Our editor looks at it again.

Gliagora writes its own items, so there is no author or uploader to notify when we remove or change one. The employer or other organisation behind the original posting can object by writing to [hello@gliagora.com](mailto:hello@gliagora.com) with the item's address and its reasons.

You can still apply to a court, as described above, or complain about the use of your personal data (see "[Complaints](/en/legal/privacy/#complaints)" in the privacy policy).

## Corrections log

We keep a log of corrections. Our editing system records every change to an item and when it was made, and keeps these entries for 1 year. We also keep each request with what we did about it (see "Personal data in your request" below). When we check a corrected fact on the original posting, the item's "Last verified" date shows that day. [TO DECIDE: M4 – whether a corrected item also shows that it was corrected, and when]

## What we do not do

- We do not give legal advice or settle disputes between you and an employer.
- We do not publish texts written by visitors or members, and we do not copy the descriptions in original postings: an item holds the key facts, our own summary and a link. If your complaint is about the original posting itself, contact the employer or the website that published it.
- We are not responsible for what linked websites say, unless we clearly present it as our own (Law No. 5651, Article 4(2)).

## Personal data in your request

We use your details to handle your request and to answer you. We do not pass them to the employer or anyone else your request concerns. We keep the record of a request, and what we did, for 2 years after it is closed.
