## In short

We store in your browser only what is strictly necessary for the site to work, so there is no cookie banner. If you never start signing in, we set no cookies. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.

## What we store

Every item in this table is set by gliagora.com itself (first-party cookies) and is never used to track you.

| Item | For | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `__Host-gliagora-session` (member session cookie) | Members | Keeps you signed in | 30 days from when you signed in; signing in again starts a new one |
| `__Host-gliagora-signin` (sign-in cookie) | Anyone who starts signing in | Protects signing in with Google, ORCID or an email link against misuse; for an email link, it lets us warn you when the link is opened in another browser than the one where you asked for it | 15 minutes; deleted once you are signed in |
| payload-token (editor session cookie) | Editors of Gliagora | Keeps an editor signed in to the editing area | 2 hours after it was set or last renewed |
| payload-theme | Editors of Gliagora | Remembers an editor's choice of a light or dark editing area | 1 year |

The two member cookies cannot be read by scripts, are sent only over encrypted connections and only to gliagora.com itself (HttpOnly, Secure, and a name starting with `__Host-`). They are sent when you follow a link to Gliagora from another site, so that you arrive signed in, but not with forms sent from other sites (SameSite=Lax). The session cookie holds a random code; we keep only a scrambled form of it, so our database alone signs nobody in. The sign-in cookie is encrypted: only our server can read it.

The editor session cookie cannot be read by scripts, is sent only over encrypted connections and is not sent when you arrive from another site (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict). The editing area also keeps some working data in the editor's browser storage, such as a copied field. A copied field stays there until the editor copies something else or clears the browser's storage.

Legal basis: for the member session cookie, our agreement with you under the [terms of use](/en/legal/terms/) (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c); GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); for the others, our legitimate interest in a secure, working site (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f); GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). ## What we do not store

- **Analytics cookies:** none. Our statistics tool, Umami, runs on our own server and counts page views and page speed without cookies. It stores nothing in your browser (see "Visitor statistics" in the [privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy/)).
- **Third-party cookies:** none. Our public pages load nothing from other websites: fonts, map tiles and spam protection run on our own server.
- **Spam protection:** your browser solves a small puzzle (ALTCHA) and sends the answer with the form. ALTCHA sets no cookie and stores nothing else in your browser.
- **Local storage:** our public pages store nothing in your browser's local storage (localStorage). Dark mode follows your device's setting. If we ever add a light and dark switch, your choice will be kept there and listed here.

## Why there is no cookie banner

The EU ePrivacy Directive (Art. 5(3)) lets a website store information in your browser without asking if this is strictly necessary for a service you asked for. The cookie guide of Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Authority ({tr: Çerez Uygulamaları Hakkında Rehber}) likewise requires no explicit consent for such cookies. Everything on this page is of that kind. [TO DECIDE: B14 – no cookie banner under ePrivacy Art. 5(3) and the Turkish cookie guide]

## Controlling cookies

You can see, delete and block cookies in your browser's settings. If you block cookies from gliagora.com, you cannot sign in, as a member or as an editor. If you delete the session cookie, you are signed out, but your account and saved items stay.

## Changes to this page

When what we store changes, we update this page. For an important change (a new purpose, a new recipient, a transfer abroad or new duties for members), we email members, put a dated notice on this page 30 days before the change applies, and mention it in the next issue of Glia Notes. Smaller edits only get a new date on this page.
