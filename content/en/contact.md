## What Gliagora is

Gliagora | Neuroscience (gliagora.com) is a hand-picked neuroscience hub, published in English first. Over time, we will bring positions, events, funding, papers, news and resources together in one place. For now, we offer positions, a world map and Glia Notes, the monthly Gliagora newsletter.

For each position, we publish a listing with the key facts, a short summary in our own words and a link to the employer's page. On this page, a **position** is a job, a **listing** is our page about it and the **employer's page** is the original advertisement on the employer's website.

We charge no fees, and no one can pay to have a listing shown or promoted. At present, we carry no advertising or sponsorship.

## Not an employment agency

We publish information about open positions. We are not an employment agency. We do not act for employers or applicants, and we do not act as an intermediary in job placement. In Türkiye, anyone who acts as an intermediary in job placement, including online, needs permission from the Turkish Employment Agency (İŞKUR) under Law No. 4904.

- We list only positions that employers, such as universities, institutes or companies, advertise on their own websites. We do not list positions advertised by recruitment agencies.
- We do not collect CVs or applications, we do not match people to positions and we do not charge anyone a fee.
- Employers cannot add listings to Gliagora directly. Anyone can [suggest a listing](/en/suggest/), and our editor decides whether to publish it.
- You apply on the employer's page. Always check the details there: if our listing and the employer's page differ, the employer's page is correct.

## How to ask for a correction or removal

Tell us if something we published is wrong or out of date, or if it affects your personal rights (for example, your privacy or your reputation). You can:

- use the **Report a problem** link on the listing or page;
- fill in the form on our notice and takedown page, our page for correction and removal requests;
- email [hello@gliagora.com](mailto:hello@gliagora.com).

Include the link to the page, what is wrong and what you would like us to do. Employers can ask us to update or remove their own listings in the same way. If you use the form on the notice and takedown page, you get an automatic email as soon as we receive your request.

If content affects your personal rights, you can ask us to remove it (Law No. 5651, Article 9). You can also apply directly to a criminal judgeship of peace (sulh ceza hâkimliği) to have the content removed or access to it blocked. [TO DECIDE: check against the current text of Law 5651 Art. 9(1).]

We reply to requests about your personal rights within 24 hours. We usually decide within 72 hours. If we remove a listing or page, it goes offline at once. If the problem can be fixed, we may correct it instead.

## How to make a request about your personal data

You can ask to see your personal data, get a copy of it, correct it or delete it, or object to how we use it. Requests are free. We answer within 30 days. If the EU GDPR applies to you, we answer within one month, even when that is shorter than 30 days.

- **If you have a Gliagora account:** download your data or delete your account at any time in your account settings.
- **If you receive Glia Notes:** unsubscribe with one click from any Glia Notes email.
