## What Gliagora is

Gliagora is a curated, English-first hub for neuroscience. It brings together positions, events, funding, papers, news and resources, and tags them all with one shared set of subfields.

Gliagora currently offers:

- job listings, with filters and "closing soon" badges;
- a world map of institutions with open positions;
- institution pages based on data from the Research Organization Registry (ROR);
- Glia Notes, the monthly Gliagora newsletter, and its public archive;
- optional member accounts;
- a form to suggest a listing;
- search.

We are adding the other sections one step at a time.

We call each thing we publish an "item", for example a job listing. Every item has:

- a link to the original posting, usually the employer's own page;
- the key facts, checked on the original posting;
- a short summary in our own words;
- a "Last verified" date.

Our editor approves every item before it is published. Software, including AI tools, may help us draft summaries, but a person always checks the facts against the original posting. Our sources and editorial policy explains how we work.

No one pays to appear on Gliagora. We do not publish paid placements.

## Information only, no guarantees

### Always check the original posting

Gliagora publishes information only. It is not professional advice of any kind.

We check facts on the original posting before we publish them, and we show when we last checked. But employers and funders change their pages, and we can make mistakes. So:

- Always read the original posting before you apply or make plans.
- If the original posting and Gliagora differ, trust the original posting.
- Only the employer or funder sets deadlines, eligibility, pay and conditions.

Our "days left" count runs until midnight at the end of the deadline day, in the institution's own time zone. If we do not know that time zone, we use the last time zone on Earth to reach midnight (UTC minus 12 hours, called "Anywhere on Earth"). A listing closes on Gliagora no more than one hour after its deadline.

## We are a publisher, not an employment agency

Gliagora is a publisher. It is not an employment agency, a recruiter or an intermediary between employers and job seekers.

Turkish Law No. 4904 says that anyone who acts as an intermediary in job placement, including online, needs permission from İŞKUR, the Turkish Employment Agency. The same law forbids publishing job ads from intermediaries that do not have this permission. Gliagora does not act as an intermediary. That is why Gliagora:

- publishes only listings that come from employers themselves, such as universities, institutes and companies;
- does not publish listings placed by recruitment agencies;
- does not accept applications or CVs;
- does not collect data about candidates or match candidates with positions;
- does not charge fees to job seekers or employers;
- does not let employers post listings themselves.

Every listing links to the employer's own page. You apply there, under the employer's own terms and privacy notice. We do not copy personal contact details from postings. If you have a question about a position, contact the employer through the original posting. The employer alone decides whom to hire.

If you are an employer and want a listing updated or removed, see "Corrections, removal and problems" below.
